EXTREME DRIVE REAL - ITS_MK_PLAYS (v8)
Cars: BMW M4, FERRARI, DEFENDER (CAR button). COLOR changes the paint.
Controls: GAS, BRAKE/REVERSE, HAND BRAKE (drift), NITRO, CAM (chase / interior / far / bumper), RESET, HORN, EXIT/ENTER (walk around).
PC: WASD/arrows, Space handbrake, Shift nitro, C camera, R reset, H horn, 1 car, 2 color, E exit/enter, F fill petrol.
INTERIOR VIEW: press CAM once. Dashboard, seats, steering wheel and a live digital cluster (speed, fuel, nitro). Drag the screen to look around.
MAP: tap the round minimap to open the full map (tap again to close; the game pauses while it is open).
FUEL: the car will not run without petrol. Drive to a red petrol pump, stop beside it and HOLD the FILL PETROL button. Free, Rs 104.90/L, 50 L tank.
WORLD: 10 km x 10 km map with smooth roads, 3 four-lane highways (median barrier, guard rails, lamps), a river, a lake, villages, a downtown and a stunt park.
PERFORMANCE: automatic quality governor lowers/raises render resolution to keep the frame rate smooth.
Goal: collect the blue ring checkpoints for +20 seconds and score.
Offline/APK: download https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js to libs/three.min.js.
Credits: BMW M4 model (Blend Swap #81957, CC-BY 3.0); Low Poly Man Rig (Blend Swap #89406, CC-BY 3.0). Keep js/m4data.js and js/pdata.js.
